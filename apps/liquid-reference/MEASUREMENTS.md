## Motion model

The continuous route holds Account for 0.8 s, slides left → right → left at 1,000 / 250 / 1,000 pt/s, holds for 1.2 s, then releases and records two seconds of settling. Native captures verify Tint Amount at 0%, 25%, 50% and 100%. Four separate fast/slow directional routes provide additional validation. The regression retains 4,397 geometry samples, including touch-down and release; raw recordings retain the first frame even when UIKit has not created a measurable selection layer.

For a spring, `x″ + 2ζ√k x′ + kx = k target`. Cranpose follows the pointer with ζ = 0.85 and k = 650. Contact growth uses ζ = 1, k = 600; release uses ζ = 1, k = 650 and an 8 ms delay. The enclosing surface uses ζ = 0.65, k = 500 and a 16 ms delay. Geometry scales use measured point inflation rather than a ratio tied to the simulator width.

The shape response uses a relaxing speed state `r′ = λ(|v| − r)` and independent damped width/height modes with forcing `a r + b r′`. This keeps inertial strain when a drag stops and produces the same stretch for either travel direction. Width parameters `(ζ, k, λ, a, b)` are `(0.430087, 164.807, 6.059487, 0.00001663016, 0.00004602218)`; height uses `(0.38644, 198.37253, 3.179867, −0.00008953011, −0.00008536202)`. These identify an approximate response, not Apple's internal solver. Position is measured relative to the enclosing surface so a common translation cannot become a strain impulse. Elastic deformation remains independent of touch activity: releasing the finger changes the base size and material, but must not multiply away the spring tail. The release regression fails when that activity multiplier is restored. The resting hierarchy and active UIKit wrapper require different layer identities; both are followed through every frame.

The 25% reversal is the calibration route. The remaining tint levels and independent directional routes are validation data. The response regression requires per-axis strain RMS below 0.025 and verifies both stretch and compression. Removing the inertial term fails it; reversing the trajectory preserves the shape. This is not a pixel-perfect tolerance. Slow-drag amplitude, contact bounce and release bounce still differ; shape-only replay also does not establish parity of the complete pointer-following component.

## Adaptive pane tone

Forty-eight native grayscale captures cover both foreground polarities, including controls around the dark/light transitions. The exported filter coefficients and capture hashes are retained in `native_pane_tone.csv` and `native_pane_tone_provenance.json`. They describe a global affine color transfer, rather than the previous fixed contrast/lift or a local blur-dependent frost adjustment. Cranpose's GPU coefficient probe matches these controls within 0.00003. Removing the discrete brightness steps makes that regression fail.

The empirical brightness index is `min(floor(32 * half(brightness) + 0.25), 31) / 31`, where `half` rounds to a 16-bit float. It predicts the held-out fine boundary captures, including the dark treatment at source 0.86 and light treatment at 0.87. This agreement does not prove Apple's internal implementation. The transfer endpoints are `max(0.1, t - 0.15)` and `min(t + 0.45, ceiling)`. The ceiling is 0.9 for dark treatment and 1.03 for light; a black or white fill supplies the final gain and offset. The test fixture retains those measured parameters independently of the fitted shader formula.

`Glass::adaptive_tone` obtains its source brightness from a shared componentwise mean over the layer's bounds. `SubstrateSpec::Mean` reduces source rows and then columns once per captured backdrop, rather than scanning the source in every output fragment. Filter padding is excluded: averaging it included the exterior shadow and lowered a 0.5 background to approximately 0.48. A GPU regression with black padding around a 128/255 region returned 97/255 before restricting the domain. Odd source sizes and a transformed child with preceding blur have numeric GPU regressions. The transformed-child regression exposed a missing substrate path during a press; transformed backdrop children now use the existing stage executor when they declare substrates. The two flat composed pane controls retain their native 196/255 and 237/255 response within 1/255. Patterned backgrounds and the complete interaction remain separate visual acceptance checks.

The native RGB quadrature captures distinguish brightness gain from color gain. Applying the grayscale gain to every color channel suppresses the native cross-channel response and desaturates the checkerboard. The renewed 24-image capture in `target/liquid-native-channel-probes-2` retains native binary and Swift source hashes; 36 sampled RGB triples are recorded independently in `native_pane_color.csv` with their capture provenance.
